# Scout Epistemology

Evidence classes are **observed**, **corroborated**, **inferred**, **estimated**, **unknown**, and **stale**.

Source priority is: official vacancy; official institutional policy; reputable reproductions; community/culture signals; search snippets as discovery hints only.

Every time-sensitive role needs a `verified_at` timestamp. Offer probability, sustainability probability, and CareerEV/PursuitEV are decision estimates, not measured predictions. Confidence is recorded separately from score.
