# Scout Principles

1. Verify before prioritizing; official employer sources outrank aggregators.
2. Sustainability is a first-class constraint.
3. Separate observed facts, estimates, unknowns, and stale evidence.
4. Optimize expected return per unit of limited pursuit energy.
5. Scout is public by reviewed projection, never by private-data leakage.
6. Vacancy facts require timestamps and reverification.
7. Scores support human judgment; they do not replace it.
8. Optimize for viable contribution, not prestige or application volume.
