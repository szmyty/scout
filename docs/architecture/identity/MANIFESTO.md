# Scout Manifesto

A job search should not require repeatedly rediscovering the same market, remembering every deadline, or spending scarce attention on low-value opportunities.

Scout treats career discovery as an information system: search widely, verify carefully, rank transparently, protect private context, keep the queue small, and prefer work that can remain good after the novelty wears off.

The objective is not maximum applications. It is better decisions with less wasted attention.
