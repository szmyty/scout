# Scout Experience Design

Scout should feel like a calm instrument panel, not a job-board feed. A user should see a bounded queue, understand why a role matters, see freshness/deadline risk, distinguish facts from estimates, and know the next action immediately.

Urgency may be visible, but the interface should not manufacture anxiety through gamification, infinite scroll, or artificial scarcity.
