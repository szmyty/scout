# Scout Ontology

- **Opportunity:** potentially relevant role before full verification.
- **Vacancy:** verified currently advertised role.
- **Lane:** role family such as RSE, geospatial, DevEx, scientific software, or applied AI.
- **Evidence:** source-backed fact or signal.
- **Constraint:** condition affecting eligibility or sustainability.
- **Hard gate:** constraint that makes pursuit non-viable.
- **Score:** versioned decision-aid output.
- **Confidence:** evidence strength behind an estimate.
- **Queue state:** discover, verify, research, pursue, watch, submitted, closed, skip.
- **Projection:** reviewed subset safe for Scout.
- **Application:** private pursuit record owned by Career.
